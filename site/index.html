The video editor that asks you for nothing

vdodtor is a full multi-track video editor for the Mac. No watermark. No account. No ads. It cannot go online at all — and unlike everyone else who tells you that, you can check.

Download for macOS What Pro adds

Free at 1080p, forever. macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

It cannot phone home, and that is checkable

vdodtor is sandboxed and ships with no network entitlement. Not "we promise not to" — the operating system will not let it open a socket. There is no telemetry, no analytics, not even a count of launches, because there is nothing to count them with.

Anyone can verify it, on their own copy, in about ten seconds:

codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/vdodtor.app

There is no com.apple.security.network.client in the list. A privacy policy is a sentence you have to believe; this is a fact you can run.

What it does

Real multi-track

A magnetic main track, three overlay lanes, audio lanes and eight lanes for text. Trim, split, ripple, snap — with the keyboard, if you like.

Words and shapes

Captions in five bundled typefaces with fill, outline, shadow and a background box; rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows beside them.

Stickers

Drop in an animated GIF, WebP or APNG. It keeps its own frame rate whatever the project runs at, and it loops.

Transitions and animation

Dissolve, slide, push, wipe, and a dip to black or white. Nine ways for a clip to arrive and leave, including a typewriter for captions.

Colour

Brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature and tint per clip, plus .cube looks — five bundled, and any of your own.

Speed and sound

Retime a clip with the pitch kept or the pitch shifted. Fades with a shape, a volume line you can draw on, and EQ presets.

Things worth knowing before you download

There is no updater

The same decision as the one above: an app that cannot open a socket cannot check for a new version either. Nobody will be nagged, and nobody will be told. Come back to the download page when you feel like it — the app's About panel shows which version you are on.

Nothing is ever sent, including when it breaks

If vdodtor hits a fault it writes a report to a file on your own disk, with every file path scrubbed out of it, and offers it to you the next time you launch. It reaches us only if you read it and paste it in. How to do that.

Your projects are ordinary files

Projects live in ~/Movies/vdodtor as plain JSON, and your footage stays exactly where you put it. There is no library to be locked into and nothing to export before you leave.
