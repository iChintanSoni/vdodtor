Your licence key

There is no account to recover, so the receipt is the thing to find. This page is what "restore purchases" is for a product that never asked you to sign in.

What it looks like

A vdodtor key is one long line beginning VDO1. — a short, signed sentence naming the order, the buyer and the tier. It arrived in the email that confirmed your purchase.

Entering it

  • Open vdodtor and choose vdodtor → Pro… from the menu, or press the PRO badge anywhere you meet one.
  • Paste the whole key, including the VDO1. at the front, and press Activate.
  • It is checked on the spot, on your Mac, with no network. If you are in the middle of an export sheet when you activate, the 4K you had already chosen stays chosen.

Lost it?

Email keys@vdodtor.app from the address you bought with, or with the order number, and we will send it again. There is nothing to reset and no password involved.

Moving to another Mac

Enter the same key. The licence is stored as one ordinary file in ~/Library/Application Support/vdodtor, holding the key verbatim — not in the keychain, and not scrambled — so you can also just copy it across. A receipt should be something you can find and read.

Deactivating does not use the key up. It removes the licence from that Mac and frees nothing, because nothing was ever counted.

Why a key and not an account

The key carries an Ed25519 signature, and checking that signature is the whole of the check. The half of the key pair that can write a licence is not in the app, which is why the app can verify one without being able to mint one, and why none of this needs a server.

It also means nothing can ever be revoked: whatever was signed is true forever, on every machine. So the key says as little as possible about you — no device count, no hashed email, nothing that would only inconvenience the people who paid.

Subscriptions that have lapsed

A lapsed licence is kept rather than deleted, with a fortnight of grace, and the Pro panel says whose subscription ended and when. Renewing puts the same machine straight back where it was.
