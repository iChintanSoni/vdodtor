What Pro adds

Export at 4KFree tops out at 1080p. Above that needs Pro — and above that, without it, nothing is written at all. No watermark, no shortened clip, no silent downscale. A file that came out smaller than the one you asked for is worse than a refusal.

A finished film never changes when a card expires

Every content pack registers with the engine at launch whatever your licence says. A project graded while you had Pro renders identically after a subscription lapses, on a machine that never had one, and in an export years from now.

Paying changes what you may choose, never what gets drawn. Locked looks are still listed and still shown, so you can see what is in a pack before deciding whether to buy it.

How the licence works

  • No account. You get a key. There is nothing to sign into, and nothing to sign out of.
  • It is checked on your Mac, with no network. The key is a signed sentence; vdodtor verifies the signature locally. The whole purchase works on a plane, and so does everything afterwards.
  • Nothing is re-checked while you work. Taking Pro away in the middle of an export to enforce a date that passed while you were editing is the one thing this product promises not to do.
  • No device count. A number nobody can check would only inconvenience the people who paid.
  • Deactivating does not use it up. Removing a licence from one Mac frees nothing and costs nothing — it is just gone from that machine.
